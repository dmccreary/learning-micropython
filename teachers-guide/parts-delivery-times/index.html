    Parts Delivery Times for Teachers

    This page helps you plan when to order parts for a class, a coding club, or a kit build. It shows how long parts usually take to arrive from common stores. It also shows which parts are used by the most kits in this book.

    Why Lead Time Matters

    Lead time is the number of days between placing an order and holding the part in your hand. The cheapest parts often ship from overseas, and they can take two weeks or more. If a part arrives late, students may sit with a half-built kit. A missing microcontroller can stop a whole class.

    Good planning lets you buy low-cost parts and still be ready on day one. The Sourcing Parts page has more advice on where to buy each part.

    These times are approximate

    The measured times come from one buyer's orders, all shipped to Minnesota. Your times may be different. Shipping is often slower around holidays, such as late November through early January and Lunar New Year.

    Planning Advice

    1. Order overseas parts at least three weeks ahead. Most orders arrive in about 9 days, but some take 15 days or longer. Three weeks covers most late orders and gives you time to test parts.
    2. Allow six weeks for anything you cannot replace locally. The slowest order took 43 days. This matters most for the parts marked "Overseas only," such as the Cytron Maker Pi RP2040 and the GC9A01 round display.
    3. Buy a few spares. Parts break, get lost, or arrive dead. Extra Picos, NeoPixels, and push buttons are the most useful spares, because so many kits use them.
    4. Use fast stores for last-minute Picos. The Raspberry Pi Pico is in more kits than any other part. If you are short on class day, Micro Center (same day, in store), Amazon (2–5 days), or DigiKey (1–3 days) can fill the gap.
    5. Split big orders. Order the cheap, common parts from overseas early. Then buy any missing or broken parts from a faster store close to the class date.
    6. Avoid the holidays. Order well before late November, and check for delays near Lunar New Year.

    See Sourcing Parts for recommended parts and stores.
